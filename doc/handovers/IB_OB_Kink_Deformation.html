Symmetry between the top and bottom half-barrels

The halves meet in the horizontal plane (φ = 0, π). A deformation that is the same in both halves, mirrored, has r·Δφ odd in φ (sin terms); cos terms and n0 are antisymmetric between the halves.

Rotation about R = 12 cm. dPar0 n2 stays within ~2 µm, so the IB and OB sub-tracks cross at R ≈ 12 cm: the relative motion is a rotation about that radius. The OB moves by ω(r − 12); the IB equivalent is −ω(r − 12), mostly a common shift of the three IB layers with a small differential.

Gain. ω = b / 0.855, from the response fit of dPar2 to the layer shifts (kinkResponse.C, pp B0).
